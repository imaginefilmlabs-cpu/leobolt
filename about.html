About LeoBolt

A film crew, rebuilt for AI.

LeoBolt is an AI film studio in Mumbai, working with brands and agencies worldwide. We make AI films, narramatics, animated videos and AI VFX. Every frame is made with AI, and every decision still goes through a director, a DOP and an edit, the same way a live-action shoot does.

How a film moves

Every project follows the same path. You see it and approve it at each stage, so nothing reaches final picture as a surprise.

  1. 01

    References first

    A script starts with real film and ad grabs, not prompts. We agree on the look in pictures before anything is generated.

  2. 02

    Look lock

    Cast, product and world are fixed as references, so the same face, the same bottle and the same street hold from the first shot to the last.

  3. 03

    Storyboard & animatic

    Every shot is boarded, then cut into a timed animatic with temp voice. You watch the film before it exists.

  4. 04

    Picture

    Shots are generated, reviewed and approved one at a time, with continuity tracked across the board.

  5. 05

    Edit & delivery

    Sound, grade and every ratio you need. Each film ships with an editable timeline, so your post team is never locked out.
